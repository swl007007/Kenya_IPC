## Key Insights Summary

1. **Food Insecurity is Prevalent:**
   - Over 70% of households employed at least one coping strategy in the past week
   - Fruit and meat consumption is very low (71.8% and 52.1% consumed none in past week)
   - Heavy reliance on purchased food even in agricultural areas

2. **Water Access Challenges:**
   - Most households depend on surface water (rivers, pans, dams)
   - Only 14.2% have access to piped water
   - 16.3% treat water before drinking (very low)
   - 6.9% not using normal water source due to breakdown or drying

3. **Livelihood Vulnerability:**
   - 46.2% rely on casual labour as main income source (unstable)
   - Despite being pastoral areas, only 2.8% have milk-producing animals
   - High dependence on external food purchase rather than own production

4. **Education Gap:**
   - Nearly half (49.4%) of household heads have no formal education
   - Only 4.5% have post-secondary education

5. **Gender Dynamics:**
   - 35.2% of households are female-headed
   - 71.9% of survey respondents were female (suggesting they are primary household managers)
